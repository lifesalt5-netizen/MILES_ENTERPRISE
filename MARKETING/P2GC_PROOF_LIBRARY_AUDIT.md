Effective: 2026-08-23
Rule: public claims may be reused or amplified only after the supporting source evidence is retained and reviewed.

Best use:
- GSA Zero-Sales Diagnostic landing page
- GSA_NO_SALES outbound follow-up

- engagement record;
- contract/customer identity retained internally;
- task-order evidence/date;
- engagement start/date supporting the timeline;

## Site-wide quantified claims requiring source-file validation

Current public success-story messaging contains aggregate quantitative claims. Before using them in ads, outbound, homepage proof strips, or new landing pages, MILES must retain source evidence sufficient to reproduce the calculation.

Required audit:
1. define the client cohort and denominator;
2. define what counts as a first award;
3. verify dates supporting any 6–12 month statement;
4. document how timeline-reduction percentages were calculated;
5. document the survey/source behind visibility percentages;
6. retire, qualify, or rewrite any claim that cannot be substantiated.

## Proof-card standard

Every approved proof card should contain:
- client type (anonymized if necessary);
- starting problem;
- action taken;
- measurable outcome;
- timeframe where verifiable;
- internal evidence location;
- permission status;
- approved marketing uses.

No proof card may imply a guarantee or typical result unless the evidence supports that representation.
