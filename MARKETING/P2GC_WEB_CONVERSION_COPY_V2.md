# Landing Page 1 — GSA Zero-Sales Diagnostic

## Hero
### Headline
Your GSA Schedule Should Not Be Shelfware.

### Subhead
If your company holds a GSA Schedule but is generating little or no measurable revenue through it, P2GC identifies what is actually blocking traction and gives you a prioritized activation path.

CTA: Review My GSA Revenue Gap

## Who this is for
This diagnostic is designed for companies that:
- hold a GSA Schedule but have zero or low sales;
- are waiting for opportunities instead of generating a targeted pipeline;
- are unsure whether pricing, offerings, agency targeting, visibility, or capture activity is the real problem;
- want to know whether the contract can realistically become a revenue-producing asset.

## What we examine
- current contract and offering position;
- available sales/activity signals where verifiable;
- agency and buyer alignment;
- category and acquisition-path fit;
- opportunity and eBuy relevance where available;
- competitive/incumbent context;
- pricing/positioning signals where appropriate;
- immediate activation priorities.

## What you receive
A concise diagnostic identifying:
1. the strongest likely cause of underperformance;
2. the highest-priority corrective actions;
3. the most realistic near-term revenue pathway;
4. whether deeper activation work is warranted.

## Why P2GC approaches this differently
We do not assume the answer is 'do more outreach' or 'bid more.' We start with the company’s actual federal position and verified market signals, then determine which action is justified.

CTA: Review My GSA Revenue Gap

## FAQ
### Is this another GSA compliance review?
No. Compliance can matter, but this diagnostic is focused on why the vehicle is or is not converting into real market activity and what should be worked first.

### Will you guarantee sales?
No. Government awards cannot be guaranteed. P2GC provides evidence-based positioning, prioritization, activation, and execution support.

# Landing Page 2 — Federal Revenue Gap Analysis

## Hero
### Headline
Find the Gap Between Being Government-Ready and Actually Generating Revenue.

### Subhead
P2GC maps where your company can realistically compete, what is blocking access, and which agencies, buyers, vehicles, teaming paths, or opportunities deserve attention first.

CTA: Show Me My Revenue Gaps

## Who this is for
Designed for companies that are registered or capable but still asking:
- Which agencies actually buy what we sell?
- Should we prime, subcontract, pursue a vehicle, focus on SLED, or use a hybrid path?
- Are we targeting the wrong opportunities?
- Which certifications or vehicles matter and which do not?
- What should we do first to improve our probability of revenue?

## What we examine
- capabilities and NAICS alignment;
- agency buying patterns;
- relevant awards and incumbent activity;
- vehicle/access position;
- certification leverage;
- prime/sub teaming pathways;
- live/forecast/recompete signals where appropriate;
- current positioning blockers.

## What you receive
- a clear pathway-status assessment;
- the strongest buyer/agency alignment signals;
- the most important access or positioning gaps;
- three highest-priority next actions;
- a recommendation on the most realistic route to revenue.

## What this is not
This is not a generic opportunity dump. P2GC first determines where the company can realistically compete, then uses opportunity intelligence inside that pathway.

CTA: Show Me My Revenue Gaps

# Landing Page 3 — Recompete & Vehicle Growth Scan

## Hero
### Headline
Get Positioned Before the Opportunity Becomes a Last-Minute Bid.

### Subhead
P2GC identifies relevant recompetes, incumbent exposure, vehicle gaps, and prime/sub paths early enough to decide whether your company should prime, team, position, or pass.

CTA: Run My Growth Scan

## Who this is for
Designed for established contractors and growth-oriented firms that need to know:
- which upcoming recompetes may fit;
- where incumbents may be vulnerable or entrenched;
- which vehicles control access;
- where teaming may be the higher-probability path;
- what should be positioned before a solicitation is released.

## What we examine
- relevant expiring contracts and recompete timing;
- incumbent contractor context;
- contract vehicle and access requirements;
- set-aside/certification context;
- agency alignment;
- probable prime vs. teaming path;
- strategic fit and capture priority.

## What you receive
For each meaningful path, a clear recommendation:
- PRIME
- TEAM
- POSITION EARLY
- MONITOR
- PASS

Each recommendation must be based on verifiable evidence rather than generic keyword matching.
