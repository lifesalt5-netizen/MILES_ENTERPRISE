# Autonomous MILES Priority

Primary objective: finish MILES as one autonomous end-to-end P2GC revenue operating system before adding new product scope.

Required chain:

`qualified lead -> verification/suppression -> campaign ownership -> governed Instantly execution -> reply intelligence -> qualified follow-up -> CRM/pipeline -> Calendly/meeting -> next revenue action`

Rules:
- Fix broken links in the existing chain before building new architecture.
- Runtime/dashboard work already accepted remains closed unless a new revenue failure proves regression.
- MONICA and new source discovery remain subordinate/parallel and must not displace the end-to-end finish.
- Qualified reply automation may only act on INTERESTED, MEETING, PRICING, or REFERRAL classifications with high confidence, required reply identity, sender account, and no suppression/opt-out evidence.
- Instantly reply mutations remain fail-closed unless all existing controlled-write gates are enabled.
- Final acceptance requires evidence of an eligible lead progressing through send, qualified human reply, governed follow-up, CRM/pipeline handling, and meeting path without manual queue editing or PowerShell intervention.
