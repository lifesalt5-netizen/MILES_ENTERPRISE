## Cross-product acceptance rules
1. One canonical company identity and one reconciled truth layer feed all products.
2. Unknown is distinct from zero, none, inactive, or negative.
3. Same fact cannot have contradictory values across modules without an explicit CONFLICTED/REVIEW_REQUIRED state.
4. Recommendations cannot assume a premise contradicted by the evidence.
5. Every material external claim has source, freshness, and confidence/provenance.
6. Modeled outputs are visibly labeled and never presented as confirmed facts.
7. Preview/locked inventory is truthful: reveal a few real records and lock only the known remainder.
8. Product acceptance is tested using representative companies covering no-sales, low-sales, established federal, GSA, non-GSA, sub-to-prime, and SLED/federal expansion cases.
9. No product is GREEN until code, data, runtime, UI, and sample truth all agree.
10. Full systems-go cannot be declared while any revenue-critical client product is PARTIAL, STALE, CONTRADICTED, or NOT_MEASURED.
