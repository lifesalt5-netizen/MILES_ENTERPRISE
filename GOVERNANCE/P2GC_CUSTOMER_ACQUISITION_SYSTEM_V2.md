# P2GC Customer Acquisition System V2

Effective: 2026-08-23
Owner: MILES
Governance owner: Kevin
Primary objective: reach and sustain $10,000/week in new P2GC sales without sacrificing deliverability, truthfulness, or client fit.

## Governing principle

P2GC does not lead with a menu of GovCon services. P2GC leads with verified, prospect-specific intelligence that exposes a revenue problem, access gap, timing risk, or missed pathway and then offers one clear next step.

Customer-facing language must be simpler than the system operating behind it. ORION, MILES, segmentation, vehicles, recompetes, award history, buyer intelligence, prime/sub intelligence, and SLED intelligence are the engine; the prospect initially buys a clear outcome.

## Three front-door offers

### 1. GSA Zero-Sales Diagnostic
Audience: GSA holders with no or low Schedule sales.
Problem: the company has invested in a vehicle but is not converting it into federal revenue.
Promise: identify the highest-priority reasons the Schedule is not producing and define the corrective revenue pathway.
CTA: Review My GSA Revenue Gap.

### 2. Federal Revenue Gap Analysis
Audience: SAM-registered firms, certification segments, and firms with federal capability but weak sales.
Problem: registration exists, but positioning, agency alignment, access, opportunity selection, or capture execution is incomplete.
Promise: show where the company is positioned to win, what is blocking access, and what should be worked first.
CTA: Show Me My Revenue Gaps.

### 3. Recompete & Vehicle Growth Scan
Audience: established contractors, expiring-vehicle segments, growth firms, and higher-value prospects.
Problem: likely revenue opportunities exist in recompetes, incumbent displacement, vehicle expansion, or teaming but are not being acted on early enough.
Promise: surface verified recompetes, incumbent exposure, vehicle gaps, and likely capture paths before the opportunity becomes a last-minute bid.
CTA: Run My Growth Scan.

## Required funnel

1. Segment and verify the company.
2. Attach one meaningful, verified company-specific insight.
3. Deliver an intelligence-first outbound message.
4. Offer a low-friction diagnostic/scan rather than a generic consultation.
5. Show proof relevant to that exact pain.
6. Book the strategy meeting.
7. Convert the finding into a productized paid engagement.
8. Expand qualified clients into recurring growth/capture support.

## Five pain families

All prospect-facing campaigns should map into no more than five primary pain families:

1. Registered but no meaningful federal sales.
2. GSA/VA vehicle present but underperforming.
3. Strong capabilities but weak agency/buyer alignment.
4. Upcoming recompete/expiration/vehicle risk or opportunity.
5. Qualified to pursue work but missing capture, teaming, or opportunity prioritization.

Segments may remain granular internally. The prospect should experience a simple problem/solution message.

## First-touch message standard

Every first-touch message must contain:

- a verified observed condition or data point;
- the business implication of that condition;
- one useful next step;
- one primary CTA.

Do not lead with generic statements such as 'we help businesses win government contracts.' Do not list every P2GC service. Do not fabricate opportunities, values, buyers, incumbents, or revenue potential.

### Intelligence-first message pattern

Subject: [specific issue or signal]

[First name] — I was reviewing [Company]’s federal position and noticed [verified fact].

That can matter because [short business implication]. We can run a [relevant diagnostic/scan] to show [specific output] and whether there is a realistic pathway worth pursuing.

Would it be useful if I sent the findings?

### GSA no-sales example

Subject: your GSA contract

[First name] — I was reviewing [Company]’s federal position and saw that the Schedule appears to be producing little or no measurable sales.

The issue is often not the Schedule itself; it is usually agency alignment, offer positioning, buyer visibility, vehicle usage, or the opportunity path behind it. We can run a GSA Zero-Sales Diagnostic to identify which of those is actually limiting revenue.

Would it be useful if I sent the findings?

### Federal revenue-gap example

Subject: a federal revenue gap at [Company]

[First name] — [Company] appears positioned to sell [verified capability/category] into government, but I found a gap between the company’s current federal position and the buyers/opportunities that appear to fit it.

We can map the most realistic agencies, buying paths, vehicle/team options, and immediate blockers in a short Federal Revenue Gap Analysis.

Should I send you what we found?

### Recompete/vehicle example

Subject: [Company] growth scan

[First name] — I was reviewing [Company] against upcoming federal buying activity and found a potential [recompete/vehicle/teaming] angle worth validating.

Rather than send a generic opportunity list, we can run a Recompete & Vehicle Growth Scan showing the verified timing, incumbent/vehicle context, and whether the path is realistic for [Company].

Want me to send the scan?

## Landing-page standard

Each front-door offer gets its own page with one CTA. Required page order:

1. Outcome-led headline.
2. Who it is for.
3. What the diagnostic reveals.
4. Why the problem costs money or time.
5. P2GC methodology at a high level.
6. Verifiable proof.
7. What the prospect receives.
8. CTA.
9. FAQ/risk reversal without unsupported guarantees.

### Homepage hero direction

Headline: Turn Your Government Contracting Position Into a Real Revenue Path.

Subhead: P2GC uses verified federal and SLED intelligence to show qualified contractors where revenue is realistically available, what is blocking access, and the highest-priority pathway from eligibility to awards.

Primary CTA: Find My Revenue Gaps.
Secondary CTA: See How the Pathway Works.

## Free diagnostic specification

Working name: Federal Pathway Score.

Minimum inputs: company name, website, UEI/CAGE if available, capabilities/NAICS, current contract vehicles, certifications, and contact email.

Minimum outputs:

- registration/vehicle status;
- current sales-position signal where verifiable;
- agency/buyer alignment signal;
- vehicle/access gap signal;
- recompete/forecast signal where applicable;
- prime/sub teaming signal;
- overall pathway status: READY / PARTIALLY POSITIONED / BLOCKED / NEEDS VALIDATION;
- three highest-priority next actions;
- CTA to review findings.

No score or recommendation may be represented as verified unless the underlying data has been checked.

## Proof library

P2GC must maintain at least three concise problem-based proof assets:

1. GSA/vehicle activation.
2. Opportunity/capture intelligence.
3. Agency/prime/sub or market-positioning improvement.

Each proof asset must retain source evidence for every quantified claim. Client-identifiable results require permission before publication.

## Founder-led authority engine

Weekly minimum cadence:

- 3 short videos;
- 3 LinkedIn data/insight posts;
- 1 P2GC intelligence email.

Monthly minimum cadence:

- 1 live webinar;
- 1 deeper case study, market report, or data analysis.

Preferred topics are proprietary-data topics competitors cannot easily duplicate: GSA zero-sales patterns, recompetes, vehicle gaps, buyer behavior, agency alignment, prime/sub pathways, and what ORION is finding in the market.

## Experiment doctrine

Do not optimize around opens. Select winners using downstream business outcomes.

Primary KPIs:

1. Positive reply rate.
2. Qualified reply rate.
3. Meeting-booked rate.
4. Meeting-show rate.
5. Proposal rate.
6. Close rate.
7. Revenue per 1,000 delivered emails.

Secondary health KPIs: delivery rate, bounce rate, negative/unsubscribe rate.

Test one major variable at a time. The standard experimental unit is pain family x front-door offer x segment.

## Website cleanup rule

Remove, redirect, consolidate, or noindex legacy pages that materially dilute P2GC’s current GovCon positioning. Old business-plan, generic grant, loan, unrelated website-development, or broad non-GovCon positioning must not compete with the core federal/SLED growth message.

## Sales-call standard

Every qualified call must begin with the prospect’s diagnostic evidence, not a P2GC company presentation.

Required pre-call packet:

- segment;
- verified observed problem;
- relevant diagnostic/offer;
- relevant proof asset;
- likely paid next step;
- likely expansion path if the initial engagement succeeds.

Follow-up target: within 24 hours of the meeting.
