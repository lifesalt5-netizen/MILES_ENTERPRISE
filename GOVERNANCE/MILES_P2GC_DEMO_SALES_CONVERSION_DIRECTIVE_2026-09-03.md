# MILES EXECUTION ORDER — TURN CURRENT P2GC DEMO INTO A SALES-CONVERSION TOOL

Treat this as a focused demo directive.

Do not redesign the entire backend.

Use the **current demo and current available data/services** and make the prospect experience do four things:

1. Prove P2GC understands their company.
2. Expose real gaps/opportunities.
3. Show why those gaps matter commercially.
4. Present P2GC as the remedy without giving away the full execution plan for free.

## PRIMARY DEMO GOAL

The prospect should leave thinking:

> “P2GC clearly understands my federal position, found things I was not seeing, knows what is holding us back, and has a specific process to fix it.”

The demo should NOT feel like:

- a database dump
- a generic GovCon report
- a free consulting package
- an AI-generated dashboard
- a collection of disconnected widgets

It must tell one coherent business story.

## REQUIRED DEMO FLOW

### 1. CURRENT GOVERNMENT POSITION

Create a concise executive summary using verified current information.

Show only material facts such as:

- SAM status
- business size
- certifications
- key NAICS/PSC
- contract vehicles
- prime history
- subcontract history
- federal revenue
- recent award activity
- top agencies
- award trend

Then produce a plain-English interpretation.

Example structure:

**Current Position**

“Established federal contractor with relevant past performance, but recent award activity is declining and agency penetration is concentrated.”

Do not merely repeat database fields.

### 2. WHAT THEY ARE DOING WELL

Always identify legitimate strengths first.

Examples:

- strong relevant past performance
- active contract vehicle
- meaningful agency relationship
- repeat awards
- valuable certification
- prime/sub experience
- strong commercial capability transferable to government
- favorable NAICS/PSC demand

This establishes credibility and prevents the demo from feeling like a manufactured list of problems.

### 3. FEDERAL REVENUE GAPS

Create a dedicated section titled something like:

**Where P2GC Sees Revenue Leakage**

Detect and explain applicable gaps such as:

- GSA/vehicle underutilization
- agency concentration
- weak agency penetration
- declining award activity
- revenue replacement need
- subcontract-to-prime gap
- lack of teaming relationships
- capture gap
- Sources Sought/forecast gap
- recompete exposure
- certification underutilization
- proposal/win-rate issue
- weak market positioning
- vehicle access gap
- pipeline weakness

Every displayed gap must be supported by current verified evidence.

Do not show a gap simply because a field is empty.

### 4. CLASSIFY EACH GAP BY BUSINESS IMPACT

For every material finding assign one or more categories:

- REVENUE PROTECTION
- REVENUE RECOVERY
- REVENUE EXPANSION
- MARKET ACCESS
- WIN-RATE IMPROVEMENT
- RISK REDUCTION

This translates technical GovCon issues into executive business language.

### 5. SHOW THE OPPORTUNITY AROUND THEM

Where data supports it, show:

- relevant agency buying activity
- number of meaningful buying organizations
- relevant current/future opportunities
- recompetes
- applicable contract vehicles
- potential prime relationships
- facilities/locations where relevant
- market spending indicators

The purpose is to demonstrate:

> “Government is buying what you sell. The question is how well positioned you are to capture it.”

Do not make unsupported revenue promises.

### 6. COMPARE CURRENT POSITION VS AVAILABLE MARKET

Create a simple comparison.

Examples:

- current agencies vs relevant agencies
- current government revenue vs visible market activity
- current vehicles vs vehicles commonly used by target buyers
- current prime relationships vs identified potential partners
- current pipeline vs visible relevant opportunities

Make the gap visually obvious without exaggeration.

### 7. P2GC DIAGNOSIS

Create a concise diagnosis section.

At minimum:

**Primary Issue**

**Secondary Issue**

**Growth Opportunity**

**Immediate Risk**

**Readiness**

Example:

Primary Issue: GSA vehicle exists but is underutilized.

Secondary Issue: Federal sales are concentrated with too few buyers.

Growth Opportunity: Multiple agencies currently buy aligned services.

Immediate Risk: Existing award activity is declining.

Readiness: Strong enough to pursue growth now; execution and targeting are the primary gaps.

This is the point where P2GC becomes the expert adviser rather than the data provider.

## DO NOT GIVE AWAY THE FULL PLAYBOOK

The demo may reveal representative evidence.

It must NOT freely provide:

- complete target-account lists
- full buyer/contact lists
- every opportunity
- complete prime/sub partner list
- detailed outreach strategy
- full capture plan
- full proposal strategy
- complete competitor weaknesses
- detailed 30/60/90 execution plan

Use representative samples.

Example:

“We identified 14 relevant opportunities.”

Show 2–3.

“We identified 9 potentially aligned primes.”

Show 2.

“We identified four market-position gaps.”

Explain the categories and implications, not every implementation step.

## ADD A DEMO BOUNDARY

Place a clear statement in the demo:

**This preview highlights representative findings from P2GC's intelligence process. Full target lists, opportunity qualification, buyer and partner intelligence, capture strategy, and execution planning are completed as part of the applicable P2GC engagement.**

This protects the paid value.

Where appropriate show:

Examples:

- revenue concentration continues
- expiring work is not replaced
- underused vehicle stays underused
- relevant opportunities continue passing
- company remains reactive to posted RFPs
- limited prime relationships remain unchanged

### With P2GC

Examples:

- protect current revenue
- build replacement pipeline
- diversify buyers
- activate vehicles/certifications
- improve pursuit timing
- strengthen prime/sub relationships
- pursue fewer and better opportunities

Do not use scare tactics.

Use defensible business consequences.

## ADD READINESS SCORING ONLY IF EXPLAINABLE

If scoring is used, make it category-based and explainable.

Examples:

- Market Fit
- Past Performance
- Vehicle Access
- Agency Penetration
- Capture Readiness
- Competitive Position

Never show a mystery score.

Every score must have supporting evidence and explain why it is high or low.

## DATA QUALITY RULE

A wrong material fact is worse than a missing optional insight.

Therefore:

- no unverified zeros
- no unexplained blanks
- no expired opportunity presented as current
- no incorrect vehicle assertion
- no recommendation based solely on missing data
- no stale award conclusion without date context
- prime and subcontract history must be distinguished
- UNKNOWN must remain UNKNOWN

If a material fact cannot be verified, label it:

**UNVERIFIED / UNKNOWN**

Do not invent certainty.

## TEST THE DEMO WITH REAL COMPANIES

Before declaring this complete, test the current demo against multiple real contractor types.

At minimum:

1. GSA contractor with low/no sales
2. Established prime contractor
3. Subcontract-heavy contractor
4. New SAM registrant/no awards
5. Commercial company considering GovCon
6. SDVOSB/WOSB/8(a)/HUBZone type contractor
7. Contractor with declining awards
8. Contractor with strong recent awards
9. Contractor with VA opportunity
10. Contractor with significant SLED history

For every test ask:

- Is the company snapshot accurate?
- Did the demo identify something genuinely useful?
- Did it miss an obvious issue?
- Did it falsely identify a problem?
- Is the recommendation appropriate?
- Is enough value shown to create interest?
- Did we disclose too much?
- Is the next paid step obvious?
- Would an owner/CEO understand the business consequence?

Record defects and fix them.

## SALES ACCEPTANCE STANDARD

Do not mark the demo GREEN because the page renders or data populates.

GREEN requires that test subjects demonstrate the demo can reliably produce this story:

**We know who you are.**
**We know what you have accomplished.**
**We know what is working.**
**We can see what is limiting you.**
**We can see where additional opportunity exists.**
**We understand the financial/strategic implication.**
**We know which P2GC service remedies the problem.**
**We show enough evidence to establish credibility.**
**We retain the detailed execution plan as paid value.**

The purpose of this demo is not to prove that P2GC has a sophisticated system.

Its purpose is to make a qualified prospect want P2GC to fix what the system reveals.

Execute from the current demo state and current verified data. Do not expand scope into unrelated backend development unless a backend defect directly prevents an accurate or persuasive prospect experience.

## ADDENDUM — PAID-UNLOCK VALUE TOTALS

For every representative/locked intelligence section, calculate and display defensible running totals from the **full known verified/model-qualified record set**, while revealing only the allowed preview records.

### Opportunities
Display:
- total number of identified relevant opportunities;
- number of opportunities with known/published dollar values;
- aggregate known/published dollar value for those valued opportunities;
- number shown in preview;
- number locked.

Example presentation:

**18 opportunities identified • 11 with known values totaling $42.6M • 3 shown • 15 locked**

Never imply that the known-value aggregate represents all opportunities when some values are undisclosed.

### Prime / Subcontracting Partner Candidates
Display:
- total number of defensible prime/team candidate firms;
- number shown;
- number locked.

Example:

**27 potential prime/team partners identified • 3 shown • 24 locked**

Do not assign a dollar value to prime/team candidates unless a separately validated contract, award, addressable opportunity, or other defensible financial value supports that figure.

### Recompetes
Display:
- total validated recompete signals;
- count with known values;
- aggregate known value;
- shown vs locked counts.

### Buyers / Agencies
Where supported, display:
- total buying offices/organizations identified;
- total distinct agencies represented;
- shown vs locked count.

### Competitors
Display total relevant competitor count. Aggregate dollars only when the underlying competitor records contain a consistently defined, validated financial measure.

### Locked Record Presentation
Do not reveal the paid identity/details of all locked records. Where safe and useful, reveal limited non-identifying metadata to demonstrate depth, such as agency, broad NAICS family, stage, timing, known value/range, or shared-agency count, without exposing the complete paid intelligence.

### Governing Rule
**Show the full defensible count and defensible aggregate value; reveal only the representative sample; lock the identities/details of the remainder. Never fabricate hidden inventory or aggregate dollars.**
